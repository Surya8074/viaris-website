# Content and asset checklist

## Brand
- Official Viaris logo and wordmark (SVG preferred).
- Approved brand colours and typography.
- App icon.

## Photography
- Hero reference and approved hero photograph.
- Real people, daily commute, intercity and community imagery.
- Usage rights and publication consent.

## Product
- Authentic screens for Find a Ride and Publish a Ride.
- One-time and recurring commute scheduling.
- Intercity discovery and publishing.
- Communities.
- Never create fictional app screens or imply unbuilt capabilities.

## Trust and conversion
- Confirmed safety and verification capabilities.
- Genuine testimonials with permission.
- Official app-store URLs and QR code.
- Contact, support, privacy and terms URLs.

## Impact calculator
Confirm its comparison baseline, distance model, occupancy assumptions, expense split, fuel efficiency, fuel price, emissions factor and tree-equivalence benchmark before publishing outputs. Label results as estimates and tree comparison as an analogy; do not imply actual trees were planted.
