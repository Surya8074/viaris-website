# Viaris design principles

## Brand story
**Same roads. Different people. One journey.**

The website should feel like a premium mobility and community brand: human, editorial, confident and modern.

## Experience architecture
1. Hero.
2. The question: what if your journey was not a solo one?
3. Viaris brand reveal branching into Daily Commute, Intercity and Communities.
4. How it works: Find a Ride and Publish a Ride within one unified platform.
5. App showcase using real screens.
6. Benefits and impact calculator.
7. Why Choose Viaris.
8. Trust and community.
9. Closing, download, FAQs and footer.

## Avoid
- Generic SaaS layouts and repeated three-card sections.
- Decorative gradients, blobs and floating UI without a narrative reason.
- Overlong copy and unnecessary explanatory sections.
- Fake statistics, reviews, availability, safety claims or fabricated product screens.

## Visual starting point
Warm ivory, forest green, restrained fresh-green accent, charcoal typography, natural light and authentic photography. The supplied hero reference and official brand assets will determine final visual details.
